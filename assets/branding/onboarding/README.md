# Onboarding mascot illustrations

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. References: the supplied `assets/piggpath-logo.webp` for brand identity and `assets/branding/registration/wave.png` for consistent character proportions/style. The original logo and registration assets are unchanged.

Final transparent PNGs: `goal.png`, `saving.png`, and `progress.png`. Each uses a 512 px square canvas with the complete generated artwork resized into a 450 px area. WelcomeScreen displays them at 256 px, or 224 px in compact mode; narrow/short screens and enlarged text use 160 px to preserve room for content. They are decorative; the adjacent headings and descriptions provide the accessible meaning.

## Final prompt set

Shared prompt, followed by the relevant pose below:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: PiggyPath welcome onboarding illustration PNG. Image 1 is the actual app logo; image 2 is the established registration mascot. Create ONE new illustration featuring the SAME cheerful coral-pink pig mascot, same pointed ears, smiling navy curved eyes, red-pink oval snout with navy nostrils, navy mouth, navy coin slot, rounded small body, short navy hooves and curled tail. Flat 2D vector-style with clean solid fills, simple bold shapes, no glossy 3D rendering or heavy shading. Match coral #FD7F8E, navy #013060, gold #FDBE02 and green #04B979; pink is visually dominant. Square composition; centered complete artwork with even clear 12% transparent margins. Genuine transparent background. No text, numbers, lettering, watermark, border, logo tile, path/base or additional characters. Few meaningful props close to the body, readable when displayed at 176px. Keep the character scale consistent across this onboarding series. 

- `goal.png`: Pose/story: the pig proudly holds a small cream goal board with a large simple pink/red bullseye target and a navy arrow in the center. One gold coin tucked near the board. Communicates choosing a savings goal. Keep the face unobstructed.
- `saving.png`: Pose/story: the pig carefully places one gold coin into a small simple pink savings jar with a navy rim beside its body, with two more gold coins stacked nearby. Communicates saving little by little. Keep the face unobstructed.
- `progress.png`: Pose/story: the pig joyfully gestures toward a small cream board with three green ascending bars and a navy upward arrow, with one gold coin sparkle nearby. Communicates growing savings and tracking progress. No numbers or labels on the chart. Keep the face unobstructed.
